本隐私政策说明手机限时锁（亦称「Flowora」「本应用」「我们」）在您使用我们的移动应用程序及相关服务时，如何收集、使用、存储与共享信息。Flowora 定位为自律与亲友/伴侣监督工具。我们仅收集实现核心功能所必需的信息，并就平台权限保持透明。

1. 我们是谁

本应用与 Flowora 服务相关运营。官方网站为 https://global.haloutongxue.com/discipline.html。 如您有隐私相关问题，可通过应用内「反馈」功能联系我们，发送邮件至 server@haloutongxue.com，或访问我们的官方网站。

2. 我们收集的信息

视您使用 Flowora 的方式，我们可能处理下列类别的信息。我们不会出售您的个人信息。

  • 账号与身份：微信登录标识、昵称、头像 URL，以及维持登录状态所需的会话令牌。
  • 监督关系数据：您的 6 位监督码、邀请、您为监督人设置的昵称/头像，以及关系状态（「监督我的人」/「我监督的人」）。
  • 应用锁定配置：锁定规则、时间表、时长限制、所选应用令牌/标识符、您选择的解锁密码或验证输入，以及执行状态。
  • 您创建的 Safebox 内容：私人笔记、私人联系人（您输入的姓名/电话/邮箱），以及您导入的私人照片/视频。上述内容主要为您使用 Safebox 功能而存储。
  • 锁定所需的使用与设备信号：在 iOS 上，Screen Time / FamilyControls、DeviceActivity 与 ManagedSettings 授权及相关令牌；在 Android 上，无障碍服务事件与使用情况访问（PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS），用于检测前台应用并施加屏蔽。
  • 会员与商业数据：VIP/会员状态、到期时间，以及通过应用内支付流程处理的购买相关记录。
  • 广告互动数据：当您通过广告聚合合作伙伴（GroMore / Pangle 技术栈）观看激励广告（例如免费锁定时长奖励）时，可能在适用的同意框架下处理广告标识符与广告事件信号，包括在 iOS 上按要求适用的 App Tracking Transparency（ATT）。
  • 您提交的反馈：消息内容、可选联系方式与时间戳。
  • 本地偏好：语言、小组件配置等存储于设备本地的设置（例如通过本地数据库 / SharedPreferences）。
  • 技术日志：有助于保障服务稳定的基本诊断信息，例如 API 错误或执行失败。此类信息不会用于广告画像。

3. 我们申请的权限

Flowora 仅在功能需要时申请系统权限。您可在系统设置中拒绝或撤销权限；部分功能届时将不可用。

  • Screen Time / FamilyControls（iOS）：选择应用/类别，并为时间限制与锁定施加屏蔽。
  • 无障碍与使用情况访问（Android）：检测前台应用并持续执行锁定。
  • 悬浮窗（Android）：在部分机型上展示锁定遮罩页。
  • 忽略电池优化（Android）：保障锁定拦截在后台持续生效。
  • 开机相关（Android）：开机后检测无障碍服务是否仍开启。
  • 前台服务与精确闹钟（Android）：有锁规则时保活进程，并准时执行锁定。
  • 通知与全屏通知（Android）：提醒锁定状态，并在需要时拉起锁定/解锁页。
  • 照片与视频图库：选择个人头像与监督人头像；为意见反馈添加图片。
  • 照片与视频图库（Android）：将照片/视频导入 Safebox；确认后可删除图库原件。
  • 网络访问：登录、数据同步与激励广告等联网功能。
  • 跟踪（iOS ATT，如适用）：在激励广告中展示更相关的广告。拒绝跟踪不影响核心锁定功能。
  • 主屏幕小组件 / App Groups：使用共享本地数据展示小组件内容。

Flowora 的许多敏感功能依赖端侧机制：iOS 上的 Screen Time 框架、Android 上的无障碍/使用情况统计，以及用于规则与 Safebox 内容的本地 SQLite 存储。

账号、监督关系、会员状态及其他服务功能可能与我们的后端服务器同步，以便在与您登录关联的会话与设备间正常工作。我们仅传输上述功能所需的信息。产品信息发布于 https://global.haloutongxue.com/discipline.html。

我们不会声称读取您锁定的第三方应用内容。锁定使用平台令牌/软件包标识符与系统 API，而非截屏或完整抓取其他应用内容。

This Privacy Policy explains how 手机限时锁 (also referred to as “Flowora”, “the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile application and related services. We designed Flowora as a self-discipline and parental/partner supervision tool. We aim to collect only what is needed to operate core features and to be transparent about platform permissions.

1. Who We Are

The App is operated in connection with the Flowora service. Our official website is https://global.haloutongxue.com/discipline.html. If you have privacy questions, contact us through the in-app Feedback feature, email server@haloutongxue.com, or visit our official website.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Flowora, we may process the categories below. We do not sell your personal information.

  • Account & identity: WeChat login identifiers, nickname, avatar URL, and session tokens needed to keep you signed in.
  • Supervisor relationship data: your 6-digit supervise code, invitations, nicknames/avatars you set for supervisors, and relationship status (“people supervising me” / “people I supervise”).
  • App-lock configuration: lock rules, schedules, duration limits, selected app tokens/identifiers, unlock passwords or verification inputs you choose, and enforcement status.
  • Safebox content you create: private notes, private contacts (name/phone/email you enter), and private photos/videos you import. These are stored primarily for your use of the Safebox feature.
  • Usage & device signals needed for locking: on iOS, Screen Time / FamilyControls, DeviceActivity, and ManagedSettings authorizations and related tokens; on Android, Accessibility Service events and Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) needed to detect foreground apps and apply shields.
  • Membership & commercial data: VIP/membership status, expiry, and purchase-related records processed through the App’s payment flow.
  • Advertising interaction data: when you watch rewarded ads (for example, free lock-time rewards) via our ad mediation partner (GroMore / Pangle stack), advertising identifiers and ad event signals may be processed under applicable consent frameworks, including App Tracking Transparency on iOS when required.
  • Feedback you submit: message content, optional contact details, and timestamps.
  • Local preferences: language, widget configuration, and similar settings stored on device (for example via local database / SharedPreferences).
  • Technical logs: basic diagnostics such as API errors or enforcement failures that help us keep the service reliable. These are not used for advertising profiling.

3. Permissions We Request

Flowora requests system permissions only when a feature needs them. You can deny or revoke permissions in system settings; some features will then be unavailable.

  • Screen Time / FamilyControls (iOS): select apps/categories and apply shields for time limits and locks.
  • Accessibility & Usage Access (Android): detect foreground apps and enforce locks in the background.
  • Display over other apps (Android): show the lock overlay on some devices.
  • Ignore battery optimizations (Android): keep lock enforcement reliable in the background.
  • Boot-related (Android): after reboot, check whether Accessibility remains enabled.
  • Foreground service & exact alarms (Android): keep the process alive when locks are active and run locks on schedule.
  • Notifications & full-screen intents (Android): remind you about lock state and open lock/unlock screens when needed.
  • Photo & video library: choose a profile or supervisor avatar; attach images to feedback.
  • Photo & video library (Android): import photos/videos into Safebox; optionally delete originals after confirmed import.
  • Network access: login, sync, and rewarded ads.
  • Tracking (iOS ATT, where applicable): show more relevant ads in rewarded-ad features. Declining tracking does not remove core locking features.
  • Home-screen widgets / App Groups: show widget content using shared local data.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide app locking, schedules, usage limits, shields, and unlock flows.
  • Enable supervisor invitations, supervise codes, and remote accountability features you choose to set up.
  • Operate Safebox (password gate, private notes/contacts/media).
  • Maintain your account, membership benefits, and purchase status.
  • Deliver rewarded ads and related free-time rewards when you opt in.
  • Respond to feedback and improve reliability and safety.
  • Comply with law, prevent abuse, and protect users, supervisors, and the service.

5. Local Processing vs. Cloud Sync

Much of Flowora’s sensitive functionality relies on on-device mechanisms: Screen Time frameworks on iOS, Accessibility/Usage Stats on Android, and local SQLite storage for rules and Safebox content.

Account, supervisor relationships, membership status, and other service features may sync with our backend servers so they work across sessions and devices associated with your login. We transmit only what those features require. Product information is published at https://global.haloutongxue.com/discipline.html.

We do not claim to read the contents of third-party apps you lock. Locking uses platform tokens/package identifiers and system APIs, not screenshots or full content capture of other apps.
