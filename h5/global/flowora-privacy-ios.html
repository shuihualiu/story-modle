2. 我们收集的信息

视您使用 Flowora 的方式，我们可能处理下列类别的信息。我们不会出售您的个人信息。

您首次启动并同意本政策前，本应用及第三方 SDK 不会为广告或画像目的收集设备标识或使用数据；同意后亦仅在实现对应功能所必需时处理信息。激励广告相关 SDK 仅在您主动进入激励广告流程时初始化，并会按系统要求申请 App Tracking Transparency；拒绝跟踪不影响核心锁定功能。

  • 账号与身份：您选择的登录方式对应的标识与资料（例如微信登录标识、昵称、头像；或通过「通过 Apple 登录」由 Apple 提供的 Apple 用户标识及您授权的邮箱/姓名），以及维持登录状态所需的会话令牌。
  • 监督关系数据：您的 6 位监督码、邀请、您为监督人设置的昵称/头像，以及关系状态（「监督我的人」/「我监督的人」）。
  • 应用锁定配置：锁定规则、时间表、时长限制、所选应用令牌/标识符、您选择的解锁密码或验证输入，以及执行状态。
  • 本机安装标识（installId）：应用在同意本政策后于本机生成的随机标识，用于匿名建户与会话恢复；并非硬件设备序列号或广告跟踪标识。
  • 您创建的 Safebox 内容：私人笔记、私人联系人（您输入的姓名/电话/邮箱），以及您导入的私人照片/视频。上述内容主要为您使用 Safebox 功能而存储。
  • 锁定所需的使用与设备信号：Screen Time / FamilyControls、DeviceActivity 与 ManagedSettings 授权及相关令牌，用于选择应用/类别并施加屏蔽。
  • 会员与商业数据：VIP/会员状态、到期时间，以及通过 App Store 应用内购买处理的购买相关记录（含自动续订状态；付款明细由 Apple 按其政策处理）。
  • 广告互动数据：当您通过广告聚合合作伙伴（GroMore / Pangle 技术栈）观看激励广告（例如免费锁定时长奖励）时，可能在适用的同意框架下处理广告标识符与广告事件信号，包括在必要情况下于 iOS 适用的 App Tracking Transparency。
  • 您提交的反馈：消息内容、可选联系方式与时间戳。
  • 本地偏好：语言、小组件配置等存储于设备本地的设置（例如通过本地数据库 / UserDefaults）。
  • 技术日志：有助于保障服务稳定的基本诊断信息，例如 API 错误或执行失败。此类信息不会用于广告画像。

This Privacy Policy explains how 手机限时锁 (also referred to as “Flowora”, “the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our iOS mobile application and related services. The App is operated in connection with the Flowora / Discipline Lock service. Our official website is https://global.haloutongxue.com/discipline.html. If you have privacy questions, contact us through the in-app Feedback feature, email server@haloutongxue.com, or visit our official website.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Flowora, we may process the categories below. We do not sell your personal information.

Ad-related SDKs initialize only when you enter a rewarded-ad flow, and we request App Tracking Transparency as required by the system before ad SDK initialization. Declining tracking does not disable core locking features.

  • Account & identity: identifiers and profile data from the sign-in method you choose (for example WeChat login identifiers, nickname, and avatar; or an Apple user identifier and any email/name you authorize via Sign in with Apple), plus session tokens needed to keep you signed in.
  • Supervisor relationship data: your 6-digit supervise code, invitations, nicknames/avatars you set for supervisors, and relationship status (“people supervising me” / “people I supervise”).
  • App-lock configuration: lock rules, schedules, duration limits, selected app tokens/identifiers, unlock passwords or verification inputs you choose, and enforcement status.
  • Safebox content you create: private notes, private contacts (name/phone/email you enter), and private photos/videos you import. These are stored primarily for your use of the Safebox feature.
  • Usage & device signals needed for locking: Screen Time / FamilyControls, DeviceActivity, and ManagedSettings authorizations and related tokens used to select apps/categories and apply shields.
  • Membership & commercial data: VIP/membership status, expiry, and purchase-related records processed through App Store In-App Purchase (including auto-renewable subscription status; payment details are handled by Apple under its policies).
  • Advertising interaction data: when you watch rewarded ads (for example, free lock-time rewards) via our ad mediation partner (GroMore / Pangle stack), advertising identifiers and ad event signals may be processed under applicable consent frameworks, including App Tracking Transparency on iOS when required.
  • Feedback you submit: message content, optional contact details, and timestamps.
  • Local preferences: language, widget configuration, and similar settings stored on device (for example via local database / UserDefaults).
  • Technical logs: basic diagnostics such as API errors or enforcement failures that help us keep the service reliable. These are not used for advertising profiling.

3. Permissions We Request

Flowora requests system permissions only when a feature needs them. You can deny or revoke permissions in iOS Settings; some features will then be unavailable.

  • Screen Time / FamilyControls (iOS): select apps/categories and apply shields for time limits and locks.
  • Photo Library: choose a profile or supervisor avatar; attach images to feedback; import photos/videos into Safebox (this app does not delete originals from the system library).
  • Notifications: remind you about lock state and help open the unlock flow.
  • Tracking (iOS ATT, where applicable): show more relevant ads in rewarded-ad features. Declining tracking does not remove core locking features.
  • Home-screen widgets / App Groups: show Flowora widget content using shared local data.

  • Respond to feedback and improve reliability and safety.
  • Comply with law, prevent abuse, and protect users, supervisors, and the service.

5. Local Processing vs. Cloud Sync

Much of Flowora’s sensitive functionality relies on on-device mechanisms: Screen Time / FamilyControls frameworks on iOS, and local storage for rules and Safebox content.

Account, supervisor relationships, membership status, and other service features may sync with our backend servers so they work across sessions and devices associated with your login. We transmit only what those features require. Product information is published at https://global.haloutongxue.com/discipline.html.

We do not claim to read the contents of third-party apps you lock. Locking uses platform application tokens and system APIs, not screenshots or full content capture of other apps.
