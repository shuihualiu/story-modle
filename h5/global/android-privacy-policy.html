2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account information

Email address, nickname, avatar, and authorized Google account profile fields when you use Google Sign-In. A guest / anonymous identifier may be used before you link an email or Google account.

2.2 Feature data

Plans, tasks, schedules, anniversaries, habits, focus sessions, reminders, statistics, preferences, membership / entitlement status, and other content you create or use in the App.

2.3 Device and diagnostics

Device model, Android OS version, app version, network status, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push token, crash and diagnostic logs, and local notification / alarm settings needed to run the App securely.

2.4 Images and media

Avatars, plan images, and memorial-board photos you choose to upload. On Android we use the system Photo Picker when available and do not request broad media-library read access for that flow.

2.5 Shipping information (optional)

Recipient name, phone number, and shipping address — only if you voluntarily submit them for prize delivery or address management.

2.6 Purchase information

When you buy subscriptions or in-app products via Google Play Billing (managed with RevenueCat where applicable), we process purchase / entitlement status, product identifiers, and related transaction metadata needed to unlock features. Payment card details are handled by Google Play; we do not collect your full payment card number.

2.7 Information we do not actively collect

We do not actively collect contacts, SMS, call logs, precise continuous location trails, advertising IDs for cross-app tracking, or biometrics unrelated to core features, unless we obtain your explicit consent for a future feature.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide core services: authentication, cloud sync, planning, reminders, and related features.
  • Send plan due reminders via on-device local notifications / alarms; with your notification permission, send remote service alerts (e.g. when someone joins a shared plan) via FCM.
  • Process Google Play purchases and restore membership entitlements.
  • Improve stability and experience using diagnostic and aggregated usage signals.
  • Protect security: authentication, abuse prevention, and anomaly detection.
  • Respond to feedback and comply with applicable law.

4. Legal Bases (where applicable)

Depending on your region (for example under the GDPR / UK GDPR), we process personal data on one or more of these bases: performance of a contract (providing the App), legitimate interests (security, product improvement), consent (optional permissions such as notifications or photos), and legal obligation.

5. Storage, Security, Retention, and Transfers

We store personal information with appropriate technical and organizational measures (including encryption and access controls). Cloud data for the global Android edition is primarily processed via our international infrastructure (Supabase). We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy and as required by law, then delete or anonymize it.

Your data may be processed in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

6. Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may share data with service providers who help us operate the App (see Section 10), or when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in a corporate transaction where the recipient remains bound by equivalent protections.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You can manage many preferences in the App or in Android system settings (notifications, etc.).

Account and data deletion

  • Without installing the App: https://global.haloutongxue.com/account-deletion

After deletion we remove associated cloud account data, subject to any legally required retention.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age required in your jurisdiction to consent to online services without parental approval). We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Android Permissions (examples)

You can revoke optional permissions in Android Settings; some features may then be unavailable.
